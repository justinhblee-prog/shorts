## Official policy basis

TikTok's current AI-generated-content guidance requires labels for realistic AI-generated images, audio and video. It also lists prohibited AI-generated likenesses of young people under 18, adult private figures without permission, fake authoritative sources/crisis events, and false public-figure endorsements. These categories are not made permissible by adding an AI label.

YouTube's spam policy prohibits automated or synthetic mass production of repetitive content with minimal variation, and its monetization policy expects original, authentic, non-mass-produced content. YouTube's Data API exposes the `status.containsSyntheticMedia` field for disclosure of realistic altered or synthetic content.
